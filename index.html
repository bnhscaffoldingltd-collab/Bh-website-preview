B&H Scaffolding Ltd is based in Tilbury. We put up scaffolding for homeowners, builders and commercial clients, from a single house to full blocks and beamed access. Our scaffolders hold CISRS cards.

Our work

Services

  • Domestic scaffolding

    Houses, extensions, loft conversions, roofs and chimneys.

  • Commercial and block access

    Full elevations and blocks of flats, with loading bays and ladder access.

  • Roofing access and temporary roofs

    Roof access, edge protection and sheeted temporary roofs.

  • Specialist and beamed access

    Beams and bridges over entrances, roofs and other obstructions.

How it works

  1. Send us the details. Address, what needs scaffolding, photos or drawings, and dates.
  2. We agree the scope and price. We visit if needed and send a clear quotation.
  3. We erect, adapt and dismantle. Coordinated with your programme.
